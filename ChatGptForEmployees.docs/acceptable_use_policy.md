# ROMTech AI — Employee Acceptable Use Policy

**Version:** 1.1
**Last updated:** 2026-08-13

---

## 1. What is ROMTech AI?

ROMTech AI is an internal AI assistant provided by ROMTech for use by employees in the course of their work. It is powered by Microsoft Azure OpenAI and is accessed through the ROMTech AI web interface.

ROMTech AI is intended to help employees with work tasks such as drafting documents, summarising information, answering questions, writing and reviewing code, and other productivity activities.

**Why it exists.** ROMTech AI runs on HIPAA-eligible Azure OpenAI services under a Microsoft Business Associate Agreement (BAA). That agreement is the reason this tool was built: it gives employees an approved place to do sensitive work with AI, so that confidential and regulated information does not end up in public tools that ROMTech has no agreement with. Using ROMTech AI for that content is the intended behaviour, not an exception to this policy.

---

## 2. Acceptable Use

You may use ROMTech AI for:

- Work-related research, writing, and analysis
- Drafting internal documents, emails, and communications
- Summarising meetings, documents, or reports
- Writing, reviewing, or explaining code
- Brainstorming and problem-solving in a professional context
- **Work involving confidential, regulated, or sensitive information** — including protected health information (PHI), client data, and trade secrets. ROMTech AI is the approved tool for this content; prefer it over any public AI tool.

---

## 3. Prohibited Use

You must not use ROMTech AI for:

- **Personal use** unrelated to your work responsibilities
- **Generating misleading or deceptive content** intended to deceive colleagues, clients, or third parties
- **Circumventing security controls** or using the tool to probe, test, or attack ROMTech or third-party systems
- **Any activity that violates ROMTech's Code of Conduct**, employment agreement, or applicable law

**Do not put ROMTech confidential, regulated, or personal information into public AI tools** such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Copilot outside ROMTech's tenant. ROMTech has no agreement covering those services, so anything entered there leaves ROMTech's protected environment. Use ROMTech AI instead — that is what it is for.

- Your conversations (prompts and responses) are stored in ROMTech's infrastructure
- Any files you upload are retained as part of the conversation log
- Logs are retained for **[PLACEHOLDER: retention period, e.g. 90 days / 1 year]** in accordance with ROMTech's data retention policy

## 5. Security

- Do not share your ROMTech AI login credentials with anyone
- Do not attempt to access other users' conversation history
- Report any suspected security vulnerability or unexpected behaviour immediately (see Section 7)

## 6. Accuracy and Responsibility

ROMTech AI can make mistakes. You are responsible for reviewing and verifying any output before acting on it or sharing it externally. Do not present AI-generated content as your own original work without review. ROMTech accepts no liability for decisions made solely on the basis of AI-generated output.
