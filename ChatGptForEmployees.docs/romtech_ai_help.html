ROMChat — Help & FAQ

Answers to common questions about getting started.

New here? ROMChat is your internal AI assistant for everyday work — drafting, summarizing, analysis, answering questions, and more. This page covers the common questions. If you're ever stuck, you can also just ask the assistant directly in a chat.

Is it safe to put sensitive information in?

Yes. ROMChat runs on HIPAA-eligible Azure OpenAI services under a Microsoft Business Associate Agreement (BAA). That means it's approved for confidential, regulated, and sensitive information — including PHI, client data, and trade secrets. That is what ROMChat is for — keeping this kind of information inside a system ROMTech has an agreement covering.

Use ROMChat instead of public tools like ChatGPT or Claude for this kind of content. ROMTech has no such agreement with those tools, so anything you paste there leaves ROMTech's protected environment. Conversations in ROMChat may be reviewed under ROMTech's Acceptable Use Policy.

What can ROMChat help me with?
  • Drafting emails, documents, reports, and templates
  • Summarizing documents, meeting notes, and long content
  • Analyzing and reviewing information
  • Answering general business and technical questions
  • Translating between languages
  • Writing, reviewing, and explaining code
  • Creating simple diagrams, web pages, and code you can preview (see below)

What was that walkthrough when I first opened ROMChat?

The first time you use ROMChat in a browser, a short guided tour points out the paperclip (for uploading documents), the tools dropdown (File Search and Artifacts), the Fast/Standard model buttons, the sidebar, and the account menu.

It only appears once per browser, and there's no button to bring it back — this Help & FAQ page covers the same ground, so you can always come back here instead.

How do I ask questions about a document?

To have the assistant read a file and answer questions about it:

  1. Click the paperclip next to the message box.
  2. Pick your file — PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, plain text, CSV, or Markdown.
  3. Type your question about the document and send.
Tip: the paperclip turns on File Search for you automatically — there's nothing to switch on first. File Search covers the documents you've added in this conversation. If it's ever off, you'll find the toggle in the tools dropdown next to the message box.

What kinds of files can I upload?

Supported: PDF, Word (.docx), Excel (.xlsx and legacy .xls), PowerPoint (.pptx), plain text (.txt), CSV, and Markdown (.md).

Not supported: images and photos (JPG, PNG, etc.), and older Word (.doc) or PowerPoint (.ppt) files saved in the legacy binary format.

I uploaded a document but it didn't seem to read it
  • Check that File Search is switched on in the tools dropdown next to the message box — it turns on automatically when you upload, but it may have been turned off earlier in the conversation.
  • Make sure the file type is one of the supported types above — older .doc and .ppt files aren't readable.
  • Scanned or photo-based PDFs (no selectable text) may not be readable.

Which model should I use — Fast or Standard?

ROMChat Fast

The default. Quick and great for everyday tasks — drafting, summaries, quick questions.

ROMChat Standard

More powerful. Reach for it on complex, detailed, or high-stakes work.

Switch anytime using the model button at the top of the chat — it shows which model you're on. Click it and pick the other. New chats start on Fast.

Can I create diagrams, web pages, or code?

Yes. ROMChat can generate things like flowcharts, simple web pages, and code, and show them in a live preview next to your chat.

  1. Turn on Artifacts in the tools dropdown next to the message box.
  2. Ask for what you want — for example, “Create a flowchart of our onboarding process” or “Build a simple web page with a contact form.”
Tip: Artifacts only appears when you ask for something it can build (like a diagram or a page). A normal question just gives you a normal answer.

Can I have a response read aloud?

Yes. Hover over any assistant response and click the speaker icon to hear it read aloud in a natural AI voice — nothing to set up first.

Can I have a private, one-off chat?

Yes. Use Temporary Chat for a conversation you don't want in your chat history — handy for quick, throwaway questions. Temporary chats don't appear in your history, and they're deleted automatically after 30 days. Until then they're stored and logged like any other conversation, so the Acceptable Use Policy still applies.

Are my conversations saved?

Yes. Your past conversations are listed in the left sidebar, so you can return to them any time. (Temporary chats are the exception — they don't show up in the list, and they're deleted automatically after 30 days.)

Good to know

  • Responses are AI-generated — always double-check important information before acting on it.
  • ROMChat can't access ROMTech's internal systems, browse the internet, or send messages.
  • For HR, Legal, IT support, or a security concern, contact those teams directly rather than relying on the assistant.
